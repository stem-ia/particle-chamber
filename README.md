# Particle Chamber

A particle theory simulation built at a science teacher's request at STEM Innovation Academy. Students get a sealed box of noble gas atoms (neon, argon, krypton or xenon). They heat it, cool it and squeeze it, record readings in a data log, and test the particle model of matter with their own data. Guided investigations cover melting points, where the heat goes during a phase change, and pressure against volume. It runs in a web browser, and on a VR headset (Meta Quest browser) it offers an immersive mode through WebXR.

Live: https://stem-ia.github.io/particle-chamber/

One self-contained file. No login, no student data.

Built by Ben Wood, Digital Learning Coordinator, STEM Innovation Academy.
